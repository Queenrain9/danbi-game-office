# Silence Cartographer — ROADMAP v0.2

## First Build candidate

Validate the central information-risk loop with a small authored set:
- Ping vs Knock,
- deterministic Hunt/ReturnToPatrol,
- permanent reveal,
- one Alcove/Hide situation,
- one Clicker decoy situation,
- Relic pickup and return-to-Entrance success.

Use fixed authored fixtures and locked RULE tests before adding broad content.

## Second Build

Expand to all six authored expeditions, mission-select progression, grade persistence and stronger hidden-predator feedback. Re-test whether deterministic routes become memorization.

## Third Build

Polish echo readability, directional audio/haptics, accessibility, mission pacing and visual cave identity. Tune Air/Clicker numbers from human playtest evidence.

## Later / Optional

Multiple predators, procedural caves, torch/light systems, combat, crafting, persistent stat upgrades, dialogue and online modes remain outside the validated MVP until the core sound-information tradeoff survives playtest.

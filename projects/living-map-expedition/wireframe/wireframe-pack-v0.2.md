# Interaction Wireframe Pack v0.2 — Living Map Expedition
Whole-MVP coverage: Expedition Brief, Living Map Gameplay, Expedition Result; Turn Planning, Move Resolution, Wait Resolution, World Shift, Pause and success/failure Result; fog/reveal/current/alternate/recorded/occupied/Spring-consumed lifecycle; all four terrain pair classes; Scout, Record, Move, Wait and Retry. The 4×4 map is the control surface. TileCell is a reusable template whose variants preserve Path/Marsh/Ridge/Spring, A/B preview, record lock, occupied freeze and consumed Spring. HUD exposes Stamina, Ink, turn and per-turn Scout/Record limits. Invalid inputs never mutate state. World Shift visually distinguishes eligible toggles from recorded, occupied, unrevealed and special cells. Shrine success priority and exhaustion failure remain explicit.
